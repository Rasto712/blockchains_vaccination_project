# Corrections to developer_plan.pdf

The PDF has no source file in this repo, so its corrections are listed here, one line per page. Each line says where the current rule lives.

- p.1: the hour estimates (Core work and Plan with buffer columns, the person-hour totals and the hours per day) no longer apply; two members are active and the current owners are in TEAM_TASKS.md. The sentences about the earlier 54-class architecture and "Existing source files have not been rewritten by this PDF task" refer to material that is not in this repo.
- p.3: identity uses one file and one salt per registering account (`data/examples/identities/<label>.json`), not one combined fixture. The JSON shown is for reading only; the real bytes are data/examples/vaccination_record.json (269 bytes, LF), saved with the rule in data/README.md. Failed evidence is "unavailable", not "UNAVAILABLE/INVALID"; outcome names are the OUTCOME_* constants in app/models.py. The tamper demo deletes its copy; nothing is restored because the original is never written.
- p.4: "Convert durationDays once using Solidity days" means expiresAt = block.timestamp + uint256(durationDays) * 1 days; without the cast the product is uint24 and reverts from 195 days. "Repeating revocation is safe" is replaced by the revoke rules in CONTRACT_API.md: unsupported scope reverts, never granted reverts NoConsentToRevoke, already revoked is a no-op with no event, expired but not revoked is revoked with an event.
- p.5: the diagram's check order is replaced by the fixed precedence in CONTRACT_API.md (scope, registration, evidence, consent, revocation, expiry, then hash; a zero hash is HashMismatch). "Events to implement" misses MinterConfigured on ConsentRewardToken; the full list is in CONTRACT_API.md. The release rule stands and now matches disclosure.py and ARCHITECTURE.md, including the commitment comparison and the extra logged attempt when the final recheck fails.
- p.6 to p.10: the hour figures in the headings, the Core estimate columns and the Time boundary lines no longer apply.
- p.7: step 5's "different requester/scope" reward test now has an outline, testDifferentRequesterOrScopeEarnsOwnReward.
- p.8: step 5 says "unavailable", not "unknown/unavailable". Step 6's restore step is replaced by copy, change one byte, request, delete the copy. The menu runs the school and doctor access demo; the tamper and exact-expiry steps run in integration/demo_workflow.py and the Solidity expiry test.
- p.9: "Export ABI/address configuration" means addresses, chain ID and artifacts_dir in the deployment file, with ABIs read from artifacts/contracts; the live settings file is config/settings.json. Step 6 scenarios have every role act, redeploy before each N and report gas as well as time.
- p.10: step 1's remark that the report limits and presentation format were missing is out of date; the format is in REPORT_OUTLINE.md. The ownership paths are replaced by the real targets in DEV_5.md. "Every person explains their own module" becomes "each member presents the part they delivered", and the handoff is listed by dependency.
- p.11: the five-owner assumption and the per-person hour target no longer apply; owners are in TEAM_TASKS.md.
- p.12: the Consent row tests 0/1/194/195/365/366 days, and 365 must succeed. The Full workflow row follows DEMO.md: revoke (REVOKED), tamper while the doctor grant is active (HASH_MISMATCH), then a 1-day regrant and exact expiry (EXPIRED) last. The scaling table runs 1, 5 and 10 requesters with every role acting, a fresh deployment per N and mean gas per operation and role.

The remarks that the page limits and presentation format were missing, and the Sources line about where the manual came from, are out of date.
